Introduction to Car Parts

  • Engine: The heart of the vehicle
  • Transmission: Transfers power from engine to wheels
  • Battery: Provides electrical power
  • Oil filter: Filters the engine oil
  • Brake pads & rotors: Stopping system
  • Suspension: Keeps ride smooth and stable
  • Radiator: Cools the engine
  • Alternator: Charges the battery
Important: Engine oil and transmission oil are NOT the same. Always clarify which fluid the customer is asking about.

Vehicle Make and Model

Make vs. Model

Make refers to the brand of the vehicle (Honda, Toyota, Ford, BMW, etc.).

Model refers to the specific vehicle model (Civic, Camry, F-150, 3 Series, etc.).

Example: A 2023 Toyota Camry — "Toyota" is the make, "Camry" is the model.
