Module 1: States

Module 2: Time Zones

The contiguous United States primarily spans four major time zones.

Time ZoneAbbreviationExample StateDifference from ET
EasternETNew YorkSame time (reference zone)
CentralCTTexas1 hour behind ET
MountainMTColorado2 hours behind ET

🕐 Remember: When moving west across the contiguous U.S., the local clock generally moves back one hour for each time-zone boundary crossed.

U.S. Time Zones by State

⏰ Reminder: Daylight saving time (DST) is a period of the year between spring and fall when clocks in most parts of the United States are set one hour ahead of standard time. DST begins on the second Sunday in March and ends on the first Sunday in November.
